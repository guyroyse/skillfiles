## Downcrawl

This skill adapts material from **_Downcrawl_** (First Edition, 2019) by
**Aaron A. Reed** (<https://downcrawl.textories.com/>).

The textual content of _Downcrawl_ is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

### What was adapted

- The Folk creation method (Basic Concept, Appearance, Reputation, Name) and
  the Volume creation method (Themes, Terrain, Sketch, Name, Abundant and
  Scarce, Inhabitants, Remoteness, Touchstone, Opportunities), summarized and
  reworded in `references/folk.md` and `references/volume.md`.
- The Table of Folk Ideas, Table of Folk Idea Connections, Table of Folk
  Appearances, Table of Reputations, Table of Volume Themes, Table of
  Terrains, Table of Resource Types, and the Remoteness table, converted into
  the JavaScript scripts in `scripts/`.
- A selection of the book's example Folk, quoted in `references/folk.md`.

### Changes made

The rules text has been condensed and rewritten as instructions for an AI
agent. The odd/even dice columns in the Folk tables have been flattened into
lists that are picked from uniformly at random. Two-word terrain results
always combine words from two rolls. Minor wording changes were made to some
table entries.
